The Bicentenary Books

Two new publications marking 200 years of Jesus College Boat Club. Pre-sale is live now through Jesus College.

The 'Red Book' (Sixth Volume)

An update to JCBC's 'Red Book'. This is the sixth volume (covering academic years 1994–2026) in a series recording the Club's crews and their performance throughout the Club's history. If you rowed, coxed or coached during these years, your name should appear in it!

Pre-order & find out more →

'For the Love of Rowing'

A richly illustrated coffee-table book titled 'For the Love of Rowing – 200 Years of Jesus College Boat Club, 1827–2027', which brings to life the extraordinary story of JCBC through the voices of those who have shaped it into the most successful Boat Club on the Cam.

Pre-order & find out more →

Ordering

How it works

Choose your fit and size, add your jacket or trackies to the basket and check out online. You'll see the club's bank details and a personal payment reference; once you've paid, place the order and a receipt is emailed to you.

Trackies orders close at midnight on Thursday 22 October 2026; jacket orders close on Sunday 25 October 2026. Everything is picked up from the boathouse; there is no home delivery.

All proceeds go straight back into the club: new boats, blades and supporting our rowers.

Good to know

Sizing: men's/unisex and women's fits. Size guides for the jackets and the trackies.

Collection: pick up from the Jesus College Boathouse; we'll let you know when your order is ready.

Questions? Email the club.
