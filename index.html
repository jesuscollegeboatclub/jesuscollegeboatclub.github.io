Welcome

Welcome to Jesus College Boat Club

Most of our members learn to row in their first term through our novice programme, then move into senior crews. If you've rowed before, you can join the seniors straight away. Several Jesus rowers trial for the University each year, with financial support from College.

There's plenty going on off the water too: bar nights, crew pastas and a black-tie boat club dinner at the end of each term.

Our boathouse is a five-minute cycle from College accommodation, with over 30 boats and a Level-4 head coach. Generations of Jesus crews have trained here, helping make JCBC the most successful college boat club in Cambridge.

The Fairbairn Cup

We've organised the Fairbairn Cup since Steve Fairbairn founded it in 1929. College crews from Cambridge, Oxford and Durham, along with their alumni, race down the Cam at the end of Michaelmas term. The 2026 races also mark the start of our bicentenary celebrations.
