Before you race

Steering the course

Fairbairns can be a tricky course to steer, particularly as there are corners you are unlikely to have tackled at race pace before. Talk to senior coxes at your club for advice on your race plan and cornering.

All coxes, and the bank parties of novice crews, must read the Coxes' Notes and the Rules and Regulations before racing. Maps of the start marshalling circulation pattern and both finish areas are below.
