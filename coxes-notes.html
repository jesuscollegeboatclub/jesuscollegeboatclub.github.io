Coxes' Notes

Advice for steering the Fairbairns course, start and finish marshalling, and the detailed circulation-pattern map.

Before you race

Steering the course

Fairbairns can be a tricky course to steer, particularly as there are corners you are unlikely to have tackled at race pace before. Talk to senior coxes at your club for advice on your race plan and cornering.

All coxes, and the bank parties of novice crews, must read the Coxes' Notes and the Rules and Regulations before racing. The detailed map of the circulation pattern within the Start Marshalling Area is in the Coxes' Notes.
