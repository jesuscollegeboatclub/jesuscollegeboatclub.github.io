Meet the Committee

Leading the club

A word from the President

President 2025–26

Emma Tabernacle

Hi! I'm Emma, and I am the JCBC President. I'm a third-year Chemistry PhD student, and this will be my sixth year rowing with the club after learning as a novice during my undergraduate degree (apparently I never figured out how to leave).

Last year I had the privilege of serving as Lower Boats Captain for the second time. Highlights included captaining four boats in May Bumps, including the first ever W6 VIII, and seeing three novice crews enjoy a hugely successful Michaelmas term. I've loved watching complete beginners gain confidence, make lifelong friends, and discover a sport that becomes a defining part of their time in Cambridge.

As the largest boat club on the Cam, we can offer opportunities for every level of rowing. Our first boats are consistently competitive, with several rowers trialling for CUBC each year — and we're just as proud of every novice's first outing and every lower-boat bump.

What has kept me at JCBC for six years isn't just the rowing: it's the community, the dedication of our coaches and committee, and the support of generations of alumni. I'm looking forward to building on that as we celebrate our bicentenary.
